## 2026-09-04 — Leadership Interview

**Participants:** Lionel Carapia, Hunter Szymborski, Estella Hageman  
**Source:** [Interview transcript](interviews/09-04-2026-LC-HS-EH.pdf)

### Observations

- **F:** Unit accountability information is currently stored across multiple spreadsheets and SharePoint files.
