## 4. Definition of finished
- New Users can download the app and create an account to log in and out. 
- Users can create and view Calendar Events with accountability trackers showing required present personel, location, uniform requirement, and additional information regarding the event (i.e Concept of Operations, Operational Order, Fragmentary Orders, Army Publication Documents, Deliberate Risk Assessment Worksheets, Medical Plan, Packing List, etc.)
- Group Chats are able to be made at the Team, Squad, Platoon, Company, and Battalion level to maximize the efficiency of chain of command and communication flow of orders. Each Group chat will have a priveledged Admin/Leadership role (i.e Team Leader, Squad Leader, Platoon Leader, Company Commander, Battalion Commander) which will be able to post BackBriefs and required information for the echelon. 
- Rank and status are able to be applied to users to give specified permissions and restrictions to prevent bypassing the chain of command. (i.e PFC unable to communicate with LTC directly)
